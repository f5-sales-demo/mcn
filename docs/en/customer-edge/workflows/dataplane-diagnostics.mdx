title: Traffic arrives but does not get through

For a site that is `ONLINE` with BGP established. If routes are the problem, start
with [BGP](../bgp-diagnostics/).

<Steps>

1. **Is anything being discarded, and why?**
   [`dropstats-non-zero`](../../commands/network/dataplane-drops/). Run it twice
   while reproducing the problem: these are lifetime totals, so a non-zero counter
   means nothing on its own and a *rising* one means everything.

2. **Find the specific connection.**
   [`flow-l-match`](../../commands/network/dataplane-flows/) with the address, or
   address and port. The action on the flow is the answer: `F` forward, `D` drop, `N`
   NAT. A flow present with action `D` is a deliberate policy decision, and sends you
   to policy rather than to connectivity.

3. **No flow at all?** The traffic is not reaching the data plane. Confirm the data
   plane has claimed the interface you expect with
   [`vif`](../../commands/network/dataplane-forwarding/), and compare its counters
   against what [`ip`](../../commands/network/ip/) reports. Traffic handled by the
   data plane never reaches the kernel, so the two views legitimately differ — but
   the data plane showing zero RX on the interface you expect does not.

4. **Flow present and forwarding, still not arriving?** Check where it is being sent:
   `rt` for the route, then `nh` to resolve the next hop. A next hop of type `Drop`
   is a blackhole, and a `Tunnel` next hop puts the tunnel itself in scope.

5. **If the path is through a tunnel**, confirm it is both up and carrying bytes with
   [`ipsec-statusall`](../../commands/network/ipsec/). A tunnel established for hours
   with near-zero counters is up and unused, which points back at routing.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Argo and Linux keep separate state">
Most confusing data-plane symptoms come from reading the kernel's view and assuming
it is authoritative. `ip` shows what Linux believes; `rt`, `nh` and `vif` show what
forwards traffic. When they disagree, the data plane is what moves packets.
</Aside>
